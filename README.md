## Reflection (Relevance of Reflection — 30 points)
### 1 Challenges I experienced
- During the EDA and correlation activities, some of the challenges I encountered included dealing with missing or inconsistent data, which sometimes affected the accuracy of my initial insights. It was also difficult to identify which variables were truly relevant to the analysis, especially when the dataset contained many features that seemed similar or unclear. Interpreting correlation results posed another challenge, particularly when distinguishing between meaningful relationships and those that might be misleading or due to outliers. Visualizing the data correctly and choosing the right type of plot sometimes took trial and error, as the wrong visualization could make patterns harder to see. Overall, these challenges helped me realize the importance of proper data cleaning, understanding variable context, and carefully analyzing correlations before making conclusions.

### 2 Possible reasons why these happened
- The challenges I faced during the EDA and correlation process mainly happened because the dataset was not perfectly clean or structured, which made it harder to interpret patterns right away. My limited experience with selecting the right visualizations and statistical methods also contributed, as I sometimes used plots that did not clearly show the trends I expected. Certain correlations were influenced by outliers or uneven data distribution, making the results seem confusing or inconsistent. These challenges occurred due to a mix of data quality issues, unfamiliarity with some techniques, and the natural complexity of interpreting real-world datasets.

### 3 What do I need to do better next time?
- Next time, I need to improve my workflow by spending more time understanding the dataset before starting the analysis, especially the meaning and purpose of each variable. I also need to practice choosing the most appropriate visualizations to make patterns easier to see and avoid misinterpretation. Strengthening my skills in data cleaning, handling outliers, and interpreting statistical results will help me analyze correlations more accurately. So, I should take a more systematic approach, checking assumptions, validating results, and reviewing my steps carefully—to ensure more reliable EDA process.
